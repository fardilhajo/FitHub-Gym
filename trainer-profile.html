Biography

With over 8 years of experience in the fitness industry, I am dedicated to creating personalised training programmes that respect each client’s individuality. My approach combines exercise science, advanced biomechanics, and in-depth knowledge of sports nutrition.

I believe that true success lies in building sustainable habits and transforming one’s mindset. It is not merely about physical results, but about creating a healthy and lasting relationship with exercise and well-being.

Specialising in strength training, functional rehabilitation, and advanced periodisation, I have helped more than 300 clients achieve their goals, from competitive athletes to individuals seeking to improve their quality of life.
